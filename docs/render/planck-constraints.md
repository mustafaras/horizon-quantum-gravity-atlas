# S21 — Planck frontier and quantum tetrahedron

The Planck module combines published scales and model-dependent limits in one reduced-wavelength map. The two axes are in metres and electronvolts. The diagonal is the conversion `E = ħc/L`; the coordinates are not evidence that every experiment probes both quantities independently. Markers below the plotted energy range are clipped to its lower boundary, and the selected detail identifies this. Hatched marker guides are visual locators, not two-dimensional exclusion contours. The hatched corner means no *direct* Planck-energy experiment; the Fermi bound is an indirect test of a particular propagation model.

The listed results include the actual torsion-balance separation range; the LHC centre-of-mass machine energy; a CMS ADD quantum-black-hole threshold exclusion; IceCube neutrino laboratory energies and cross-section measurement; an XENONnT candidate mass and associated WIMP cross-section limit; a Fermi linear-dispersion bound; the revised GWTC-3 graviton mass bound; a non-rotating reference radius computed from EHT's M87* mass estimate; the CMB sound horizon and Hubble radius in base ΛCDM; and a BICEP/Keck inflation energy limit derived under single-field slow-roll with the Planck scalar amplitude. The result list supplies the limits, uncertainties, model caveats and links to primary sources. None is a combined fit.

Each entry's hatched guide is drawn at its quoted extent when the source states a range: Eöt-Wash spans 52 μm–3 mm and IceCube 6.3–980 TeV, both converted to the other axis through `E = ħc/L`. A single quoted scale, such as the LHC machine energy or a bound, gets a symmetric locator box; it is a pointer to the value, never a two-dimensional exclusion contour.

The current GWTC-3 publication, arXiv:2112.06861v3 / Phys. Rev. D 112, 084080 (2025), quotes `2.42 × 10⁻²³ eV/c²` at 90% credibility; its first preprint quoted `1.27 × 10⁻²³`. Fermi Table 2(a) gives `1.19 E_P`, rounded to 1.2 in the plan. The map extends the requested upper axis bounds to `2 × 10²⁸ eV` and `2 × 10²⁶ m` so Planck energy and the Hubble radius fit. The full CMB and GWTC-3 values remain available in the selected detail when their equivalent energies fall below the lower boundary.

The ladder's actual existing input is a length slider, so its value is published as a typed ConceptBus event and drives the plot crosshair; ordinary page scrolling does not change the selected length. Experiment chips work with pointer, touch and keyboard, and the small in-plot module markers navigate to related atlas modules.

The spin figure shows the area of a single link and the volume eigenvalues of a four-valent quantum tetrahedron with four equal spins. The chosen Immirzi parameter is γ = 1, matching the convention in Bianchi & Haggard, Phys. Rev. D 86, 124010 (2012), not a measured value. The code diagonalizes the real-symmetric equivalent of their finite tridiagonal squared-volume matrix, retaining degeneracies. For spins 1/2, 1 and 3/2, tests compare normalized levels with their Appendix C (0.310; 0 and 0.620; 0.537 and 0.993). Each figure's vertical window is derived from the levels it actually draws for the current maximum spin j, so the highest stem keeps its headroom at every slider setting. The existing 3D node sizes remain a Cayley–Menger visual proxy; they do not depict these volume-operator eigenvalues. LQG is labelled conjectural.
