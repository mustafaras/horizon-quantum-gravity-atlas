## Known limits

- **README loop.** A 24 s WebM records on CI through the publishing studio, but it captured the wrong canvas and washed
  out its translucent layers, so it is not shipped (D-211). The README carries the hero still.
- **Browsers and backends.** Every browser check ran on Chromium with a software rasteriser and WebGPU disabled, as CI
  does. The WebGPU paths are exercised only where an adapter exists (the native-boundary check in CI); Firefox and
  Safari were not run.
- **Timings are software-rasteriser timings.** They compare runs on one machine; they are not hardware claims.
- **An intermittent skip-link test** in the Print theme under a long run (D-206), passing alone.
- **F-17 and F-27** as above, and the three capture observations of the manual review.
